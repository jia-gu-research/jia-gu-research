# Jia Gu

I am interested in empirical accounting and finance. My projects use financial disclosures, experimental data, and text to study how firms and individuals behave.

## Accounting research coding sample

**[Cash-tax payments in loss firms](https://github.com/jia-gu-research/sec-cash-tax-research)** follows a reported tax payment from an SEC filing into an empirical sample. The first notebook checks XBRL facts for duplicates and reconciles cash taxes across jurisdictions. The second examines which firms report positive cash-tax payments despite a pretax loss. Of 520 loss observations, 141 have complete data for the descriptive Logit analysis.

[Data construction and quality checks](https://github.com/jia-gu-research/sec-cash-tax-research/blob/main/notebooks/01_data_construction_QA.ipynb) · [Sample construction and preliminary analysis](https://github.com/jia-gu-research/sec-cash-tax-research/blob/main/notebooks/02_preliminary_empirical_analysis.ipynb)
